Breathing Exercise

Take a short pause and follow this simple breathing exercise.

Slow Breathing

Step 1: Breathe in slowly for 4 seconds.

Step 2: Hold your breath for 4 seconds.

Step 3: Breathe out slowly for 4 seconds.

Step 4: Rest for 4 seconds.

Repeat this cycle several times at a comfortable pace.

Stop the exercise if you feel uncomfortable or light-headed.
